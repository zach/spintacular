# Art and audio direction

The look and sound of the game. `presentation.md` says what must be on screen; this document says what it should look and sound like. The product owner chose this direction from three.

## The direction: carnival midway

The game looks and sounds like a night at the fair: a striped prize wheel ringed with bulbs, painted signs, ticket stubs and a brass band. It's loud, warm and cheerful, and it fits the name: Spintacular is a spectacle.

The rules keep their words. The player still loads a truck with cargo and delivers it; on the midway, the truck is the show's wagon and the cargo is its prizes.

- **Shapes:** flat color, bold outlines and hand-painted sign lettering. No pixel art.
- **Palette:**
  - **Ground:** a deep night blue behind the stalls.
  - **Frame:** tent-stripe red and cream, for everything that frames the game.
  - **Bulb gold:** kept for what matters most: the quota line, warnings and inspections.
  - **Cargo:** four colors, each with its own shape (circle, triangle, square, diamond), so color is never the only signal.
- **Type:** a fat slab display face for headings and big numbers, like a fairground sign, and a plain companion for text. Numbers are the stars: big, tabular and always in the same place.
- **Wheels:** prize wheels on stands, each ringed with bulbs, with a leather flapper for a pointer. A wedge that would spoil the load goes dark and gets a cross.
- **The truck:** the show's wagon, side-on, with prizes stacking on its bed as they land. The quota is a painted fill line on its side, with a row of bulbs that light as the load nears it. The wagon sinks on its springs as the load grows.
- **Items:** tickets and trinkets pinned along the top of the stall, in the order they apply.
- **The shop:** a prize booth. Offers hang on its back wall, and the bag of wedges is a tray on the counter.
- **Inspections:** the fair inspector's notice, pasted across the round's header.

## Motion

- **The spin:** the wheel slows over about a second, and the flapper's last few clicks carry the tension. The bulbs chase around the rim while it turns.
- **A delivery:** each scoring step lights a row of bulbs and stamps its number on a ticket, faster as the total grows.
- **A spoiled load:** the bulbs cut out and the prizes slide off the wagon. It's quick and it isn't softened.
- **Everything else stays still.** Motion marks a spin, a save, a spoil or a payout, and nothing else.

## Sound

- **The wheel:** the flapper clicking past pegs, slowing with the wheel.
- **Risk:** a snare roll that tightens as a wheel's chance to spoil rises, so the player hears the danger before reading it.
- **The quota:** a rising slide whistle as the load nears it, and a bell when it's reached.
- **A save:** a cymbal catch and a gasp from the crowd.
- **A spoil:** a sad trombone cut short, then a beat of silence.
- **A delivery:** a ticket machine rattling out the payout, speeding up with the total.
- **Music:** a fairground band in layers: a calliope alone in the shop, with a player joining for each cargo in the truck, so the tune fills as the load does. A full truck brings the whole band in.

## Risks to manage

- **It's close to other wheel and slot roguelikes,** CloverPit among them. What sets Spintacular apart is the wagon and the press-your-luck load, so the wagon, the quota line and the spoil odds get the most screen and the most polish.
- **It mustn't read as a casino.** There are no chips, no cash-out and no slot reels. The fair is a show, and the player is running it.
- **Bulbs and stripes compete with numbers.** Decoration stays on the frame; the wheels' wedges, the odds and the payout steps stay plain and high-contrast.

## Directions not chosen

| Direction | For it | Against it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Road signs and loading docks:** highway-sign green, stencilled steel wheels | Built on the haul's own subject; reads at a glance | Dry next to a name like Spintacular |
| **Tabletop:** wooden wheels, chunky tokens and a felt table | Matches the board games the rules come from | Harder to make odds read at a glance |
