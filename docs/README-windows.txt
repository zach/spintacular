SPINTACULAR
===========

Load the wagon one spin at a time. Every spin makes the load worth more, and
one bad spin spoils the lot.

To play: run spintacular.exe. It needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a
graphics card with Direct3D 12.

Controls
  Mouse       Click a wheel to spin it. Click DELIVER to deliver the load.
              Hover anything for an explanation. Drag items to reorder them;
              right-click an item in the shop to sell it.
  1 to 6      Spin that wheel.
  Space       Deliver.
  Esc         Pause, settings, save and leave.
  F11         Full screen.
  F1          The tuning panel (for trying balance numbers; not part of the game).

A run saves itself after every spin and every purchase. Saves, unlocks and
settings live in %APPDATA%\Spintacular\Spintacular.

The balance numbers are in assets\data\tables.json.

Fonts: Alfa Slab One and Lato, under the SIL Open Font License (assets\fonts).
The engine is composed with Automap from SDL3, SDL_shadercross, Dear ImGui,
miniaudio, stb, GLM, spdlog and nlohmann/json.
