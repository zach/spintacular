How it works

Click a wheel to spin it. What it lands on goes in the truck. No repeats: a wedge that lands twice in one trip, or cargo with a value already in the truck, spoils the whole load. Wedges marked with a cross would spoil it, and each wheel shows its chance. A delivery pays points × mult: every cargo adds its value in points and 1 mult, so bigger loads pay much more. Three or more of one color, or a run of consecutive values, add mult. Reach the quota for double. Six different values is a full truck. Miss a round's score and the run ends.
